# Software Studio redesign

## Purpose

Help OU students quickly understand the program, see real student work, and join.
Use the supplied context brief as background, not page copy.

## Core copy

Hero direction updated after the user's screenshot review: preserve the current photo, dark overlay, large white logo on the left and copy/actions on the right. Preserve the headline **Be a Part of Entrepreneurship and Innovation!** Shorten the supporting copy rather than replacing the opening identity.

Supporting line: A two-semester program where OU students turn ideas into real software startups.

Hero actions: Sign Up and Invest, matching the current site. Startup exploration follows immediately below the hero. Later joining sections may use Join Software Studio and Join Discord.

Essential reassurance: All majors welcome. No idea, team, or coding experience required.

## Homepage

1. A bold, concise introduction with the two actions and authentic student/product imagery.
2. Real startups, using Sanity records, with cohort browsing and clear product links.
3. A compact journey: Pitch → Team up → Validate → Build → Launch → Grow.
4. A short participation section: developers, designers, founders, marketers; biweekly sessions and team work between meetings.
5. A final join action and compact contact/footer area.

Announcements should be a small timely update area, not long posters and full articles ahead of the program explanation. Preserve access to the archive.

## Supporting pages

- About: mission, OU Innovation Hub connection, leadership.
- Startups: current teams and previous cohorts, product details and public links.
- Program/Join: commitment, how to participate, and concise FAQs.
- Updates: announcements and existing blog content.

## Visual direction

Modern incubator, not a generic club template. Restrained crimson, charcoal and white; confident typography, generous space, actual student photos and product screenshots. Use short headings and paragraphs. Avoid decorative dashboard interfaces, invented metrics, heavy card grids, stock imagery and excessive gradients. Keep navigation simple and mobile access obvious.

All currently used assets must remain in use in the appropriate pages and archives. See `redesign-asset-inventory.md` for the current published media references and locked hero assets. Homepage curation must not drop assets from startup profiles, About, announcements or blog articles.

## Accuracy and content

- Preserve supplied startup names, member spellings and descriptions; use short previews with full details available.
- Do not invent funding, outcomes, testimonials, formal mentorship, fees or academic-credit details.
- Treat cohort schedules as examples unless confirmed.
- Leave live Sanity content untouched while developing. Do not edit published content solely to suit a preview.
- Keep joining links centralized, with current recruitment status verified before launch.
- Improve Sanity fields for product URLs and project imagery where needed; maintain compatibility with existing records.
